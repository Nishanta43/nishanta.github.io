About Me

I am a dedicated researcher with a background in social science, specializing in qualitative research, health technology assessment, and cost-effectiveness. My work focuses on quantifying the impacts of environmental factors and climate change on public health—such as the future burden of Dengue, Melioidosis, and Zika—to drive sustainable, evidence-based adaptation strategies and Universal Health Coverage.
